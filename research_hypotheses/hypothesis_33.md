## Hypothesis

 The performance of large language models like GPT-3 and GPT-4 is significantly influenced by the methodological issues associated with training on large web corpora, which in turn impacts their few-shot learning capabilities on specific datasets. Specifically, the hypothesis posits that increasing the scale of the language model does not linearly improve its few-shot performance on challenging datasets due to inherent biases and noise present in web corpora. 

To test this hypothesis, one could design an experiment where a series of smaller language models (with gradually increasing parameters) are trained on curated, high-quality datasets that are free from web-specific biases and noise. The performance of these models in few-shot learning scenarios would be compared against GPT-3 and GPT-4.
